WONDERFUL CREATURES

Get to know wildlife

Here are a few amazing animals and some reasons their protection matters.

FOREST

Bengal tiger

A powerful big cat that helps maintain balance in its ecosystem. Protecting connected forests is important for its future.

GRASSLAND & FOREST

Asian elephant

Elephants help shape habitats and disperse seeds. Safe migration routes and space to live are essential.

WOODLAND

Spotted deer

Also known as chital, this deer is part of the food web and is found in Indian forests and grasslands.

SAVANNA

Life on the plains

Grasslands support grazing animals, predators, birds, and insects. Healthy plains depend on a delicate natural balance.

PROTECT WHAT YOU LOVE

Small actions can help wildlife

Keep natural places clean, respect animals from a distance, avoid products made from wildlife, and learn about conservation in your community.

  • 01 Reduce waste and avoid littering.
  • 02 Respect animals and their habitats.
  • 03 Share trusted wildlife information.
